## Reading this honestly

- These are real players around the rating above, so the numbers describe what people do and what works for them, not what a grandmaster would play.
- A plan label is a rough rule about pawn moves, not a full understanding of the position. "Quiet" mixes genuinely quiet games with plans made of piece moves.
- Rows marked "few games" or "too few to trust" should not be believed; the margin shown is the guide.
- Stronger players tend to pick better plans; comparing with the rating-based expectation removes much, but not all, of that effect.
